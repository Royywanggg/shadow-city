# Injury, mortality and persistent life state (Round 3)

## Scope and integration

Round 2 habits and the Encounter pipeline are present on the `codex/daily-habits` base. This work adds an optional `LifeScenarioConfigV1` and `LifeStateV1` inside the existing `SimulationStateV5`. `WorldModelV2`, citizen identities, routes, offences, witness reports, replay fingerprints and the researcher God View remain the existing systems. No separate engine or hidden scripted death is introduced. The integrated application enables life state by default; `?life=off` reproduces the older v5 configuration. Older v2–v4 and v5 records lacking `life` continue on their original paths.

The Tick order is: recovery step from the prior state; activity, routes and six-second movement; encounter/offending and existing witness reporting; qualifying physical harm; activity/social interruption; later death-scene discovery and delayed life report delivery. A harmful event completed in a Tick changes ordinary behavior beginning with the following Tick. Events created in the same Tick use the pre-harm contact trace. All outcomes use keyed `hashSeed` draws. The experiment fingerprint already includes configuration and command history, so changing life parameters invalidates a replay record.

## State and transition rules

`LifePersonV1` is a discriminated union. Alive residents have one of `unimpaired`, `impaired`, `incapacitated`; deceased residents have no functional status, no active injury IDs, and a valid death event and Tick. Death is irreversible within a run. Seeking before death reconstructs the earlier alive snapshot. Profiles, appearance, relationships, memories, offences, activities and encounter history are retained; the resident is never removed from the population array. `assertLifeStateInvariantsV1` rejects missing/duplicate entities, invalid activity/life combinations and death scenes detached from their truth event.

`InjuryRecordV1` stores victim, source offence, onset, severity, actual victim position, `surfaceId`, `levelId`, place, step-down/recovery Tick and status. A fatal injury is retained as history, not as an active injury. `DeathEventV1` and `DeathSceneV1` are separate stable truth records. The death position is read from the victim's authoritative end-of-Tick spatial state and checked against a walkable surface or connector polyline; it is never snapped to ground or copied blindly from the offender. Immediate death and injury share one Tick in v1. Delayed mortality is deferred; no fabricated physiological countdown exists.

For an offence to be eligible, the completed offence must have a real citizen target and be explicitly classified `physicalAssault`. The classification is an independent keyed Bernoulli draw conditional on target and offence severity. `serious` alone never qualifies an offence. After eligibility, injury is a separate Bernoulli draw, severity is a categorical draw, and critical injury receives a separate fatal draw. These are synthetic model assumptions, not epidemiological or clinical estimates. The configured defaults are:

| Parameter | Minor offence | Serious offence |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Physical-assault probability when target is a citizen | 0.08 | 0.36 |
| Injury probability given physical assault | 0.34 | 0.72 |
| Injury severity weights (minor / serious / critical) | 0.88 / 0.11 / 0.01 | 0.42 / 0.45 / 0.13 |

Critical injury has a separate 0.12 immediate fatal probability. Minor injury impairs movement; serious and nonfatal critical injuries incapacitate for 90 and 360 Ticks respectively, then become impaired. The step-down uses one explicit `standingUp` Tick with no world movement; normal route movement resumes on the next Tick. Recovery occurs at 360, 1,440 or 2,880 Ticks after onset (minor, serious, critical). Impaired movement speed is 0.65 of the resident's normal simulation speed. These numbers are transparent configurable synthetic parameters and have **no medical calibration**. Ordinary routines and needs do not advance while incapacitated or deceased. A serious injury interrupts the active route/interaction/intention. If the resident was partway through an edge, recovery completes the existing legal edge to its next safe node before choosing a new destination; it does not teleport. Death cancels pending routine goals and prevents later activity, travel, offending, and new social episodes. Existing social relationships and history remain queryable. Future access commands are environmental commands and still take effect; a deceased resident simply cannot act on them.

## Knowledge, observation and scene lifecycle

Objective death, a witness's perception of a harmful offence, later scene discovery, private death knowledge and an observer report are distinct records. Existing `WitnessObservation` is imported only as `witnessedHarmfulOffence`; it does not imply that the witness perceived the resulting injury or knows that death occurred. Later discovery requires a living, capable resident on the **same surface**, within 7 m, with an unobstructed segment, an attention-weighted keyed perception draw, and a scene that already existed before this Tick. Discovery creates one local observation and an opaque recognition-token claim; a believed name is available only if prior local identity knowledge supports it. Other acquaintances receive no automatic broadcast or grief response.

The scene is `present` until an explicit future cleanup mechanism is modelled (`persistUntilExplicitCleanup`). There is no automatic ambulance, police cordon, or scene disposal. Each discovery may probabilistically schedule a life-scene report after three Ticks by default. Reports are separate from offence reports and do not create another death. ObserverPort's runtime allowlist projects only rounded reported position, optional level/place, delivery Tick and `possibleDeathScene`; true victim, offender, injury, death ID and cause remain outside the projection. Researcher God View can inspect complete state in any event visualization mode. Secondary hearsay and official death notification are not implemented.

## Rendering and inspection

The existing ten CC0 Quaternius character presets and their retargeted `Idle`, `Walk`, `Run`, `SitDown`, `SitIdle`, `StandUp` clips remain the asset source; see [character animation](CHARACTER_ANIMATION_CITYWIDE.md) and [asset source/license](../public/characters/quaternius-ual-v1/SOURCE.md). There is no authored collapse, death or injured-gait clip. Close rigs sample `SitDown` from a Tick-based transition and tilt toward a resting pose; recovery samples `StandUp` during the stationary `standingUp` Tick. Medium and far tiers use the same state-driven orientation. This is a restrained **placeholder collapse/death motion**, not an approved high-quality death animation. It never writes simulation position. A selected death marker uses the actual world location and can remain visible through occluding building geometry. The portrait uses the same preset/colors/life state and seeks directly to a stable pose. `WorldAgentLayerV5` keeps deceased residents visible, including those formerly at home.

The God View `LIFE` tab shows current biological/functional status, source-linked injury/recovery/death records and coordinates. Its integrated timeline includes activities, encounters, offences and life events; selecting a spatial event seeks to its Tick and uses the three-dimensional camera target. The normal `Truth/Observed/Compare` switch does not restrict this researcher panel.

## Scientific metrics and limitations

`computeLifeMetricsV1` returns `Synthetic Simulation Results` at a stated Tick. Living/deceased/injured/incapacitated are person counts at that Tick. Injury severity counts and fatal/recovery counts are cumulative events through that Tick. Injury-to-fatal rate is fatal injuries divided by all modelled injuries and is `null` when there are no injuries. Deaths by level and deaths linked to a qualifying physical assault are cumulative. `witnessedHarmEvents` counts distinct offences with a valid witness observation; `discoveredDeathEvents` counts distinct death events discovered later. **Witnessed death** is `null` because the model does not establish perception of the death transition itself. Harm-to-death elapsed time is 0 for immediate outcomes and `null` with no deaths; delayed mortality is absent. Serious-offence count is never substituted for death count. No metric predicts a real population.

The simulation does not model medicine, hospitals, emergency response, accidents, natural death, grief, official notification, forensic investigation or disposal. Injury classification and recovery are deliberately coarse. The current visible resting pose and occlusion behavior require a dedicated asset/lighting/indoor-cutaway art pass before claiming polished death-scene presentation. The observed Chrome p95 frame interval varies near the prior 33 ms target; GPU frame time and actual VRAM allocation were not measured.
