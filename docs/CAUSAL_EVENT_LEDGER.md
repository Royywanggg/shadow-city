# Causal event ledger

The typed extension records the sequence `opportunity episode → hazard threshold → attempt → completed offence → item or life consequence → observation → report`. It does not count each link as a separate offence. `OpportunityEpisodeV1` includes negative episodes with no attempt, eligible duration, accumulated hazard, sampled threshold and an explicit reason for ending. `CrimeAttemptV1` distinguishes `failed`, `invalidated` and `completed`; only `completed` creates a truth offence. Offence IDs link to the existing injury, death, scene, witness and report records.

The recent episode summaries are capped by `maxRecentEpisodes` (default 4096); older summaries are *not* asserted to be resident in the current state. `archivedThroughTick` marks truncation. A researcher can inspect a historical Tick through deterministic session replay and recover its then-current summaries. All actual attempts, completed offences, item identity and awareness records remain in the replayable state. This is an in-memory research prototype; long multi-day runs still require measurement of attempt/history growth and may need a persistent chunk archive.

Event Explorer indexes episodes, attempts, completed offences, life outcomes, scenes, witness observations and delivered reports. Its list renders at most 200 rows at a time and offers type/place/Tick filters. Selection reveals the episode's rate, exposure, risk, threshold, item and target, plus linked attempt/outcome, victim knowledge and observer-visible reports. The inspector reads ground truth only in researcher God View; its filters and camera actions do not feed the engine.

Within a Tick, movement/contact samples and hazard update first; attempted actions recheck current possessions; witness perception follows completed offences; self-check awareness follows its due Tick; pending reports are delivered only when their availability Tick is reached. Habit feedback reads prior committed subjective memories on the next Tick. Duplicate offences are prevented by episode consumption and cooldown, and conflicting item actions revalidate possession before transfer.
