# Twilight terrain development scene

## Scope and reference

`?world=dev` is an isolated terrain and road experiment derived from the 300 m integrated Shadow City. `?world=main` continues to use `heritage-extension-v6`; the original city generator, central plaza, Galleria, building asset geometry, simulation rules and world-time lighting remain on their existing paths. The dev generator has its own version and replay fingerprint. No assets or runtime dependencies were added.

The user-provided [`Twilight Isometric City Diorama.png`](references/characters/Twilight%20Isometric%20City%20Diorama.png) was inspected. It informed the rear terrace, lower left watercourse, graded roads and short bridges. The reference also has a much more organic whole-city street pattern than the current dev scene. The dev scene is an infrastructure vertical slice; its Hero composition is **not yet approved** as a visual match.

## Data flow

| Source | Dev behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `createDevOrganicLayout()` → `generateIntegratedWorld({ baseCity })` | Moves selected dev-only road nodes, blocks, parcels, entrances and street features together before rebuilding the pedestrian graph and semantic places. The injected city must have the same seed and 300 m extent. Main takes the original generation path. |
| `deriveDevRoadCorridor()` → `placeDevCorridorParcels()` | Defines three 8 m S bends on shared road nodes, then fits twelve adjacent polygonal blocks, parcels, buildings and entrances against those same centrelines. Two constrained buildings are deterministically regenerated on narrower parcels; their IDs, kinds and place semantics remain stable. The pedestrian rings and crossing curbs follow the polygon and road graph. |
| `applyDevValleyPlan()` → `createDevValleyChannel()` | Extends a 6 m lateral river bend over 54 m between two bridges. Both flanking road centrelines, bridge nodes, nine polygon blocks, parcels, street features, terrain channel and legal pedestrian paths use one plan. The curved park surface, path and trees stay within its authoritative footprint. |
| `DevTerrainV1` | Serializes a 2 m deterministic height grid, shared curved escarpment contours, a channel with explicit `fullDepthZmm`, building pads and entries, road profiles, bridge spans and culverts. `heightAtMm()` is shared by rendering and navigation. |
| `applyDevHighlandPlan()` | Removes seven dev-only through roads before the integrated world rebuilds its pedestrian graph. The road graph remains connected around the west and east ends of the contiguous north terrace; main retains its original roads. |
| `applyDevRidgeFrontage()` | Gives the existing six-edge street along the foot of the north terrace one 5 m analytic bow. Five shared junction nodes, road centreline samples, street features and six south-side polygon block frontages move from that same curve. Unwalkable cleared terrain blocks are excluded before pedestrian derivation, so no provisional crossing is generated through the ridge. |
| `DevTerrainV1.terraceAccess` | Derives a south-side crossing, terrain-sampled stair and curved upper pedestrian path from the same fixed escarpment and road profile. `generateDevWorld()` adds a ground crossing, a capacity-limited stair connector, a distinct upper surface and six deterministic park activity slots. The renderer uses those exact paths for its treads, handrails and paving. |
| `applyDevUpperRoadPlan()` → `addDevTerraceNeighborhood()` | Adds a dev-only connected curved road edge from an original junction to the upper shelf, then eleven small parcels and buildings using the existing building generator. The new blocks have explicit pedestrian-only frontage; their sidewalk rings, indoor places and routes are derived by the integrated world before terrain elevation is applied. |
| `generateDevWorld()` | Shifts ground and indoor nodes to the terrain, builds portal treads and bridge paths, excludes the channel and escarpment from ground navigation, and emits the versioned dev `WorldModelV2`. |
| `CityStaticScene` | Converts the authoritative terrain and road profiles into indexed ground, water, road, sidewalk and bridge meshes, plus shared/instanced landscape details and building foundations. Curved four-way junctions derive fitted asphalt, paving, curb corners and lane-mark termination from the same road profiles. |

The original 300 m city remains the placement base. The dev scene clears the channel and north terrace test blocks to make terrain and bridge clearance possible. Other building IDs, local masses, windows and functional places remain; selected outer and approach buildings move with their blocks. The plaza and Galleria blocks, channel and terrace anchors stay fixed. This is a prototype exception to full building retention, not a new default map.

The first dev layout pass retains rectangular blocks and south-facing entrances, with shared road-node offsets of about 4 m inside the city and 5 m at the perimeter. Subsequent passes give twelve corridor blocks, nine valley blocks and four ridge-frontage blocks polygon footprints beside shared curved roads. Their road ribbons, sidewalk rings, crossings and navigation use the same centreline data. The café `building-block-3-4-0-0` narrows from about 37.62 to 31.63 m, and the home `building-block-3-5-1-0` from about 18.64 to 15.23 m; other retained buildings keep their IDs, role and visual language. Fixed and two alternate seed tests check road clearance, connected pedestrian paths and entrance access. Four-face entrance and interior portal support now exists, but no dev building has been assigned a non-south entrance. The hero/lookdev asset renderers still assume south-facing entry geometry, so assigning those alternate faces requires a separate visual integration pass.

The original v9 north-route fixture reached `block-2--1`; v13 clears that block for the expanded highland and now verifies a legal route to retained `block--1--1` on its west side. It is computed with `findRouteV2` and driven tick by tick through the production `moveCitizenOneTick` function; it reaches the target without a blocked step or a disconnected road edge. V10 adds a separate route from an existing entrance across the curved frontage road, up the terrace stair and along six distinct upper slots. The production movement function reaches the final slot with a continuous 3D position and no blocked step. These are route fixtures, not proof that naturally scheduled residents visited during the zero-population screenshots.

## Open architecture question

The dev-only layout pass runs **before** integrated static world construction. Local polygon blocks and the paired river corridor prove that curved roads can retain the static graph and most original buildings. Four-face pedestrian access and interior portals now exist, but asset renderers and dev placement do not yet exercise non-south entrances. The wider barriers are the inherited rectangular lattice, axis-aligned parcels, south-facing asset entry visuals and whole-block highland clearing. All five buildings originally in the four cleared channel blocks intersect the channel centreline; at least 23 old pedestrian edges cross its forbidden strip. A broad inhabited rear terrace needs coordinated road grade, building frontage and navigation recomposition. A tunnel would require explicit traversable road surfaces under nonwalkable terrain; merely raising the ground broke a stair landing in an earlier experiment. Until a citywide spatial redesign passes the Hero review, do not call this a complete organic-city conversion or use the current screenshot as evidence of reference fidelity.
